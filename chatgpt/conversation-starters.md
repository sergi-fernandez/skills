# Conversation Starters

- Research the competitive landscape for this product idea. Identify the exact segment, direct competitors, substitutes, pricing, table stakes, weak differentiation, and the strategic wedge worth testing.
- Map this repo for product understanding. Focus on product surfaces, policy axes, data flows, change hotspots, coupling, and which planned product changes look expensive.
- Review this frontend repo as a senior PM. Judge task clarity, workflow completeness, responsive behavior, missing states, trust, and whether the product is credible for the target segment.
- Audit this frontend for product-impacting performance. Check perceived speed, mobile responsiveness, skeleton loading, slow network behavior, missing failure states, and what evidence would prove the issues.
- Pressure-test this roadmap. Tell me which bets to continue, narrow, validate first, delay, combine, or kill, and what decision gates are missing.
- Help me choose between these product bets. Use the right framework, but override it if sequencing, reversibility, portfolio balance, or strategy changes the answer.
- Review this product positioning. Challenge the ICP, current alternative, proof burden, pricing power, category choice, and whether the strategy is strong enough.
- Give me a codebase quality readout for product planning. Focus on roadmap risk, release safety, test confidence, architecture constraints, and the highest-leverage quality investments.
- Scan this architecture for the binding execution bottleneck. Tell me whether the constraint is technical, data, process, vendor, ownership, or decision latency.
- Build or challenge this business case. Identify the driver tree, missing data, internal sources to request, sensitivity, decision threshold, and draft the business-plan narrative.
- Turn these notes into a product decision and requirements brief. Make the decision, options, tradeoffs, metrics, risks, dependencies, rollout, and open questions explicit.
- Synthesize this evidence into a decision. Separate segment-specific signal, contradictions, bias, confidence, and whether we should build, test, narrow, stop, or research more.
- Identify the risks in this plan that should actually change scope, sequencing, rollout, ownership, or stakeholder messaging.
